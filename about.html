About Me

Who I Am

I am a professional AI/ML Engineer currently working at Swappl Intelect Solution, Bhubaneswar, Odisha, where I develop intelligent, scalable, and production-ready AI solutions for real-world business applications. My expertise includes Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and AI chatbot development. I have hands-on experience building end-to-end AI applications using Python, FastAPI, LangChain, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and vector databases such as FAISS, ChromaDB, and Pinecone. I am passionate about leveraging cutting-edge AI technologies to automate workflows, enhance user experiences, and solve complex business challenges. As a continuous learner and problem solver, I stay updated with the latest advancements in AI and cloud technologies while delivering innovative, efficient, and high-quality solutions.

I love turning ideas into real-world applications that create impact and solve problems — bridging the gap between research and product.

Outside of coursework, I spend most of my time shipping small AI-powered demos end-to-end — from model to a working interface someone can actually click through.
